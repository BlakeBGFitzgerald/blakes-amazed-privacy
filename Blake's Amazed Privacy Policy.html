Blake’s Amazed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Blake’s Amazed, a mobile maze game published under the Blake’s brand by Blake Fitzgerald.

1. Developer and publisher

Publisher/brand: Blake’s
Developer: Blake Fitzgerald
Privacy contact: thecraziesttimes@gmail.com

2. Information stored by the game

Blake’s Amazed is designed to operate without requiring an account, social profile, or cloud-save service. Game progress, settings, achievements, statistics, preferences and related local game data may be stored on the device using the game's local storage.

The developer does not intentionally collect your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, microphone recordings, camera images, precise location, or other directly identifying information through the game.

3. Advertising

The free version of Blake’s Amazed may display interstitial advertising provided by Google AdMob. Advertising is not displayed during active maze gameplay. The game may request advertising-related information required by the advertising service to select, measure, prevent fraud in, and deliver advertisements.

Where required by applicable privacy law, Blake’s Amazed uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage consent before requesting advertising in relevant regions. Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized advertising is available.

4. Google services

Blake’s Amazed uses Google services for advertising and in-app purchases. Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

For information about how Google handles data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

Blake’s Amazed may offer a one-time in-app purchase called Remove Ads. Purchases are processed through Google Play. The game receives the purchase and entitlement information necessary to grant the purchased feature. The developer does not receive your full payment card or banking details through the game.

6. Analytics and tracking

Blake’s Amazed does not intentionally use a separate analytics service, advertising attribution SDK, or user account system beyond the services described in this policy.

7. Children's privacy

Blake’s Amazed does not require children to provide personal information. The game does not knowingly request a child's name, address, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Advertising and consent behaviour may vary according to applicable requirements, user choices, and Google service configuration.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on the device until it is removed by the user, for example by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to how the operating system and Google services handle their own data.

If you have a privacy question or believe personal information has been processed in connection with Blake’s Amazed, contact the developer using the address above.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, services, or applicable privacy requirements change. The latest version will be made available at the public URL where this policy is hosted.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact thecraziesttimes@gmail.com.
